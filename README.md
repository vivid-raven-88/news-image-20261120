**Friday 20 November 2026**, entered in the make.fun competition
[Sum up 20 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120).

## The day in five stories

1. **AI's $2 trillion day** — Anthropic lists on the Nasdaq in the biggest
   IPO on record; shares open 34% up at $111.50, touch a $2 trillion value,
   and close 17% higher at $97.17.
2. **Three leaders, one 'peace declaration'** — Trump, Putin and Xi pledge
   no nuclear war in Shenzhen, but say nothing on Ukraine or Iran.
3. **Asia's AI fright** — Taiwan shares plunge 2,200 points; the Nikkei
   and Kospi fall 4.8% as AI-bubble fears spread.
4. **1,000 drones for Kyiv** — Britain pledges a £260m drone package as
   Russian missiles strike the capital.
5. **Fossil fuels cut from draft** — outcry at COP31 as the deal text drops
   a phase-out; Africa seeks tripled climate finance.

Also today: Pete Rose pardoned by Trump; UK–EU youth-mobility deal agreed.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the front page
`entry.png` — drawn in this repository's session — to
`/output/entry.png`, and checks the copy matches the original. It runs
with no network and writes the same bytes on every run.
